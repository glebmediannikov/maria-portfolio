Financial Education · Innovation

AltRisk Academy

Founded and developed by Maria Mediannikova, AltRisk Academy is a free interactive educational platform designed to make financial literacy and risk management accessible to high school and college students. What began as a financial risk calculator has evolved into a broader learning environment featuring interactive simulations, investment risk analysis, real-world decision-making scenarios, and educational modules covering responsible financial choices. The platform combines programming, quantitative thinking, and practical financial education to help students understand uncertainty and make informed decisions.

Leadership · Education

Financial Literacy Club

Founded and led by Maria Mediannikova, the Financial Literacy Club helps students build practical money-management skills through interactive discussions, real-world financial scenarios, guest speakers, and collaborative learning. The club explores budgeting, credit, investing, financial risk, and career opportunities while connecting students with financial professionals and educational organizations.
