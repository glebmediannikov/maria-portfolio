Summer Internship • Sustainable Design • Clean Energy • Project Presentation

Perspectives of Earth — Final Stakeholder Presentation

July 2026Framingham State University, Massachusetts

As part of the 2026 Perspectives of Earth — Team Mentorship & Internship (PETM), organized by MetroWest STEM Education and the Christa McAuliffe Center, Maria participated in a five-week collaborative project focused on sustainable energy and community-centered design.

The student team developed and presented a conceptual design for a 100% clean-energy library at Framingham State University.

The proposed library was envisioned as a welcoming space for students, faculty, and the wider Framingham community, supporting collaborative learning, community engagement, and sustainability.

The project also explored opportunities to integrate geothermal infrastructure and solar energy capabilities, demonstrating how clean-energy technologies could support both the university campus and surrounding community.

The internship concluded with a Final Stakeholder Presentation, during which the team shared its proposed design and project findings.

Geothermal Energy • Eversource • Clean Energy Infrastructure

Eversource Geothermal Energy Network — Technical Site Visit

Summer 2026Framingham, Massachusetts

As part of the 2026 Perspectives of Earth — Team Mentorship & Internship (PETM), Maria participated in an educational site visit to Eversource Energy's pioneering networked geothermal pilot project in Framingham, Massachusetts.

The visit featured technical presentations by Shawn Luz, Sustainability Coordinator for the City of Framingham, and Isabel Varela Gutierrez, Science Director at HEET, who introduced students to the engineering design, infrastructure, and operating principles of the geothermal network.

Students toured the geothermal pump house and borehole locations, gaining firsthand insight into how underground thermal energy systems can provide heating and cooling to buildings.

The experience offered an opportunity to explore one of the nation's first utility-owned networked geothermal systems and understand the collaboration between energy utilities, local government, researchers, and sustainability organizations in advancing clean-energy solutions.
